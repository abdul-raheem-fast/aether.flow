=== SLIDE 6 (ppt/slides/slide6.xml) ===
Routing parameters
Cost and latency are normalized. Unmet limits trigger a reported fallback.
06
Parameter
Meaning in the prototype
Quality score Q
Per-query estimate from the learned scoring head
Cost penalty λcost
Weight on recorded average cost per query
Latency penalty λlatency
Weight on recorded average model response time
Privacy eligibility
Only approved models can receive sensitive queries
Optional limits
Quality-score floor and estimated latency budget
Utility = quality - λcost × cost - λlatency × latency
